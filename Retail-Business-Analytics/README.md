
# Retail & E-commerce Business Analytics

## Business Problem
Analyse retail sales data to understand revenue, profitability, customer behaviour, and regional performance.

## Objectives
- Identify sales and profit trends.
- Compare product and regional performance.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Develop actionable business recommendations.
